Our Philosophy

Health, safety and peace of mind — backed by evidence

With the rise of drug-resistant "superbugs", COVID-19, MRSA, staph and mould spores, every organisation now has to balance the health and safety of its people against rapidly-changing guidance. Goldshield exists to make that easier: a patented technology, peer-reviewed on four separate occasions, that gives you an effective, safe and eco-friendly germ-control system without the guesswork.

Our Story

Born from a personal loss

In the middle of years of research on antimicrobial technology, our co-founder Tom Higgins' father passed away from a hospital-acquired infection. It drove him to eliminate the possibility of another family losing a loved one to poorly-performing disinfectants.

With his partner Ted Shlisky, and a former Dean of Harvard Medical School, the Goldshield formula was born in 2008. Continuous R&D since has extended the technology to hands, surfaces, laundry and FFP3 masks — and the family continues that work today.

The Science

A nano-scale bed of spikes

Goldshield bonds to any surface or textile, protecting against microbial contamination, drug-resistant biologics, mould and mildew — without exhaustive re-application.

The structure resembles a nano-size "bed of spikes". Goldshield carries a nitrogen molecule that gives it a positive electrostatic charge; most germs are negatively charged, so they're drawn to any surface Goldshield has treated — then physically ruptured on contact.

The bond formed is covalent: Goldshield becomes part of the surface, and won't wash or rub off during normal cleaning. Each spike can neutralise multiple germs before reaching capacity — typically around 90 days, which is why protection lasts for weeks, not minutes.
